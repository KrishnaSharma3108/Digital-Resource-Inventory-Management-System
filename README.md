# Digital Resource & Inventory Management System
## Problem Statement
Develop a centralized resource management system to digitally track institutional purchases, inventory, dispatches, bills, and financial records.
## Key Features
- Purchase Management
- Inventory Management
- Resource Dispatch Tracking
- Bill Management
- Financial Record Management
- Centralized Digital Records
- Search and Reporting
## Objective
To replace scattered physical records and spreadsheets with a centralized digital system for efficient resource management.
